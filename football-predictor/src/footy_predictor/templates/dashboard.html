How the predictions work

Each league gets its own Dixon-Coles goals model: every team has an attack and a defence rating, fitted on up to three seasons of results with recent matches weighted more heavily. Where bookmaker prices exist, the model's expected goals are blended with the goals the market implies.

All four markets come from one table of scoreline probabilities, so they always agree with each other. "Fair odds" is 1 ÷ probability: a price above it means the bookmaker is paying more than the model thinks the tip is worth.
